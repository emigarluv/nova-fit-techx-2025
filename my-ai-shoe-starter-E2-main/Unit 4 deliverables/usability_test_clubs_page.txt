Clubs page:

Participant introduction:

In this activity you will be interacting with the clubs page of a workout app. Your persona is someone who would like to be a part of a workout group but is not sure what your options are or where to start. Please speak out loud to convey your thoughts as you interact with the prototype. 


Discover your fitness club options.
Figure out which club is most ideal for you.


Participant 1:
Confused on where to click first
Easily searched location using search bar at top

Participant 2:
Figuring out what to do with sidebar
Wanted to use text box at the bottom
Confused with what the search results were displaying

Which parts were frustrating for you
Participant 1: I wasn't sure where the specific details of the club were
Participant 2: I was trying to figure out what I should use the bottom textbox for

Which parts were you unsure about?
Participant 1: I wasn't sure where I could find the price of the fitness club
Participant 2: I wasn’t sure how to see the details

Is there anything that left you feeling like you were missing something?
Participant 1: I wish I could clearly see the price of each fitness club
Participant 2: I want to know how far the club is from my house not just that it's in my city

Conclusion:
The bottom textbox was confusing for participants so we removed it. I added more detailed wording to the 
prototype so that test participants would be able to more clearly know what they’re looking at. Participant 
1 said that the price of a fitness club would be something important that they’d want to find easily, so 
in the details we added a price section where the user can clearly see how much they need to pay. I also 
made the title of the club text a bit larger so that the user's eye is drawn to it after participant 2 was 
confused about the search results.
